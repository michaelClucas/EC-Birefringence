# arXiv:2509.13654 - Diego-Palazuelos & Komatsu, "Cosmic Birefringence from the Atacama Cosmology Telescope Data Release 6"

Version read: v2 (14 Apr 2026), full text via arxiv.org/html/2509.13654. Code: github.com/pdp79/act_dr6_analysis (footnote 2).

## Data
- ACT DR6 official cross array-band power spectra and covariances (act.princeton.edu/act-dr6-data-products, footnote 1). Spectra from Louis et al. arXiv:2503.14452. (Sec. II)
- Observations 2017-2022, 19,000 deg2 footprint; power spectra computed on 10,000 deg2 after Galactic/point-source masking (extension of Planck 70% Galactic mask, Louis et al. Sec. C.2). (Sec. II, III)
- Five polarized array-bands: PA4 f220 (182-277 GHz), PA5 f090, PA5 f150, PA6 f090 (77-112 GHz), PA6 f150 (124-172 GHz). PA4 f150 excluded (failed ACT null tests). PA4 f220 polarization was not used by the ACT team; this paper includes it. (Sec. II)
- Multipole ranges (Table 1, bin centres): PA4 f220 1000.5-7525.5 (39 bins); PA5 f090 1000.5-7525.5 (39); PA5 f150 800.5-7525.5 (43); PA6 f090 1000.5-7525.5 (39); PA6 f150 600.5-7525.5 (47). Cross-spectra use the higher b_min. Total 995 x 2 data points for EB+TB (Sec. III).
- Auto-spectra of the same band are kept (DR6 spectra are cross-split, so no noise bias) (Sec. III).
- Spectra used: EB and TB (plus EE, BB, TE in the data vector model). Template CMB spectra: best-fit LCDM to ACT DR6.

## Method
- Posterior Eq. (1) with Gaussian prior on alpha_i; semi-analytic iterative small-angle method (refs de la Hoz+ 2022, Diego-Palazuelos+ 2023). Matrices A, B in Eqs. (2)-(3).
- Rotation model: C_l^{TB,o} = sin(2 beta) C_l^{TE}, C_l^{EB,o} = (1/2) sin(4 beta)(C_l^{EE} - C_l^{BB}) (Sec. I).
- Mixing matrix including I->P leakage gamma_i (Eq. 4): rows (T, E, B); E^o = gamma_i T + cos(2alpha_i+2beta) E - sin(2alpha_i+2beta) B; B^o = gamma_i T + sin(2alpha_i+2beta) E + cos(2alpha_i+2beta) B. So (E+iB)^o = e^{2i(alpha+beta)}(E+iB) at gamma=0.

## Miscalibration angle alpha handling
- alpha is NOT determined from Galactic foreground EB (unlike Minami-Komatsu Planck analyses). Priors on alpha_i come from the ACT optics model (Murphy et al. arXiv:2403.00763): sigma(alpha_PA4) = 0.09 deg, sigma(alpha_PA5) = 0.09 deg, sigma(alpha_PA6) = 0.11 deg; rotation within passband < 0.01 deg; frequency dependence of dichroic detector response and later processing not modelled (Sec. II).
- Three prior sets (Sec. II, Fig. 1): independent (Pi^ind); 90% correlated within a PA (Pi^PA, baseline); 90% correlated per frequency (Pi^nu).
- Galactic dust TB/EB neglected: at l >= 600 the spectra are CMB+noise dominated after masking, and beta here does not rely on foregrounds to break the degeneracy (Sec. III).

## Headline numbers (degrees, 68% CL)
- Baseline (Pi^PA, EB+TB, with I->P leakage marginalised): beta = 0.215 +- 0.074, 2.9 sigma from zero. Abstract; Table 3; Sec. VI.
- Table 3 (with leakage): Pi^ind 0.208 +- 0.059; Pi^PA 0.215 +- 0.074; Pi^nu 0.216 +- 0.073. chi2 2027/2030/2029 for 1979 dof; PTE 22.1/20.7/21.4%.
- Table 2 (no leakage): Pi^ind 0.204 +- 0.058; Pi^PA 0.212 +- 0.072; Pi^nu 0.212 +- 0.072. chi2 2031/2034/2032 for 1984 dof.
- alpha_i (Table 3, Pi^PA): PA4 f220 0.025 +- 0.085; PA5 f090 -0.014 +- 0.072; PA5 f150 0.023 +- 0.072; PA6 f090 -0.030 +- 0.077; PA6 f150 -0.029 +- 0.076.
- Residual leakage |gamma_i| <= 0.08% (Table 3, Sec. V); shifts beta by <= 0.07 sigma.
- TB only (Pi^PA): beta = 0.254 +- 0.131 (Sec. IV).
- Pipeline validation, EB only, no PA4 f220, fitting psi_i = alpha_i + beta: psi = 0.108 +- 0.064 (PA5 f090), 0.316 +- 0.059 (PA5 f150), 0.118 +- 0.074 (PA6 f090), 0.185 +- 0.060 (PA6 f150); weighted mean 0.192 +- 0.032 (stat only) vs ACT team 0.20 +- 0.03 (Sec. IV). PA5 f150 - PA5 f090 = 0.21 +- 0.09.
- ACT team (Louis et al. 2503.14452) mean angle including optics systematic: 0.20 +- 0.08 (Sec. I, VI).
- Uncertainty grows from 0.032 (stat) to 0.058 (Pi^ind) and 0.072 (Pi^PA, Pi^nu) once alpha priors are propagated (Sec. IV). The beta error is dominated by the optics-model prior, not statistics.
- Combined with WMAP+Planck (assumed independent): with Eskilt & Komatsu 2022 -> 0.264 +- 0.058 (4.6 sigma); with Cosmoglobe reanalysis (0.26 +- 0.10, Eskilt+ 2023) -> 0.231 +- 0.059 (3.9 sigma) (Sec. VI). Checked: inverse-variance combination reproduces both numbers.

## Frequency dependence
- beta is assumed frequency independent (achromatic); separate alpha_i per array-band. No beta(nu) fit.
- Per-array psi_i above show f090 lower than f150 within PA5 and PA6 (pattern noted by the ACT team, Fig. 9 of Louis et al.); the Pi^nu prior was introduced to test this. The PA5 alpha discrepancy is 2.0 sigma (Pi^ind), 1.1 sigma (Pi^PA), 1.9 sigma (Pi^nu) (Sec. IV). No Delta chi2 preference among priors.

## Systematics caveats stated
- Unresolved ACT systematics (PA5 90 vs 150 GHz angle discrepancy) prevent strong cosmological conclusions (Abstract, Sec. VI).
- Optics model does not include frequency dependence of dichroic response or data processing.
- Beam/I->P leakage tested with gamma_i; small effect.
- Dust EB neglected (justified above).
- Calls for artificial-calibrator experiments (BICEP3, CLASS, Simons Observatory) for confirmation.

## Model / Lagrangian conventions
- No Lagrangian is written in this paper. The only sign statement: positive beta is a clockwise rotation, negative counterclockwise, over the whole sky (Sec. I). See `conventions_beta.md`.
- No beta <-> Delta phi formula in this paper.

## Anisotropy
- None. Isotropic beta only.
