# arXiv:2510.07928 - Balkenhol, Coerver, Reichardt, Zebrowski, "Probing Anisotropic Cosmic Birefringence with Foreground-Marginalised SPT B-mode Likelihoods"

Version read: v2 (14 Nov 2025), full text via arxiv.org/html/2510.07928. Journal: Open Journal of Astrophysics.

## Data
- SPT-3G D1 BB (Zebrowski et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.02827): two years, 1500 deg2, 95/150/220 GHz, mask matched to BICEP/Keck region; 32 <= l < 502, five bins per cross-spectrum, 30 multi-frequency band powers; 7 nuisance params (3 polarisation calibrations, dust amplitude/two spectral params, radio galaxies). (Sec. 2.1)
- SPTpol BB (Sayre et al. 2020, arXiv:1910.05748): three seasons, 500 deg2 inside SPT-3G field, 95/150 GHz; 52 < l < 2301, seven bins per cross-spectrum, 21 band powers; 14 nuisance params. Foreground model changed for compression: free Poisson terms replaced by SPT-3G radio-galaxy model with spectral index fixed at about -2.65. (Sec. 2.2)
- The two data sets are analysed separately (correlation not quantified).

## Method
- CMB-lite compression (Dunkley et al. 2013) to foreground-marginalised CMB-only band powers; SPT-3G sampled with NUTS (200,000 samples), offset log-normal likelihood Eq. (3); SPTpol Gaussian. Validated: r limit within 1-2%, A_LT shifts <= 0.2 sigma.
- CMB-lite assumes achromatic CMB in K_CMB; signals with non-CMB SED (e.g. Faraday rotation) cannot be constrained with these compressed likelihoods (Sec. 2). Birefringence considered here is achromatic, so this does not apply.
- Model Eq. (6): D_l^BB = r D^r + A_LT D^lens + A_CB D^CB. CB template for Planck 2018 best fit from Lonappan et al. 2025 (based on Namikawa 2024 modified CLASS, exact non-thin-LSS calculation, Eq. 4).
- Definition Eq. (5): L(L+1) C_L^{alpha alpha} / (2 pi) = A_CB [rad^2] (scale invariant).
- Priors: uniform, non-negative on r, A_LT, A_CB unless stated. Optional "A_LT APS prior" N(1.010, 0.016^2) from Qu et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.20038) joint SPT-3G/ACT/Planck lensing. Cobaya MCMC, R-1 < 0.02. (Sec. 3.1)

## Isotropic rotation / miscalibration alpha
- Both SPT analyses apply EB-leakage deprojection that removes any global rotation (cosmological or instrumental). So these data have zero sensitivity to isotropic beta by construction, but keep sensitivity to anisotropic rotation (Sec. 3.1). No beta measurement in this paper. No Galactic EB modelling (BB only).

## Headline numbers (SPT-3G D1 BB lite)
- No A_LT prior (Eq. 7): r < 0.26 (95%); A_LT = 0.69 +- 0.18 (68%); A_CB < 1.2e-4 (95%).
- With A_LT APS prior (Eq. 8): r < 0.26; A_LT = 1.007 +- 0.016; A_CB < 0.53e-4 (95%). Also in abstract and Sec. 4.
- Replacing APS prior with Planck-based A_LT prior: A_CB < 0.55e-4 (footnote 5).
- Allowing unphysical A_CB < 0 (no A_LT prior): A_CB = (-1.13 +- 0.94) x 1e-4, 1.2 sigma from zero; 89% of posterior mass at A_CB < 0; 95% region width 3.7e-4; sigma(A_LT) = 0.31; rho(A_LT, A_CB) = -0.87 (Sec. 3.2). With A_CB >= 0: rho = -0.59 (SPT-3G) vs -0.93 (SPTpol).
- The tight limit comes partly from SPT-3G bin 4 scattering low and bin 3 high; authors say scatter is within statistical expectation (Sec. 3.2, Fig. 6).

## SPTpol numbers
- With APS prior: A_CB < 1.6e-4 (95%), posterior peaks at A_CB > 0 (Sec. 3.2).
- With Planck-based A_LT prior: 1e4 A_CB < 1.70 (multi-freq, original FG model), 1.62 (multi-freq, updated radio model), 1.64 (lite) (footnote 4). Within 15% of literature.

## Cited anisotropy bounds (as quoted in this paper)
- Lonappan et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.13154), BICEP/Keck + POLARBEAR + ACT (+SPTpol per its abstract), Planck-based A_LT prior: A_CB < 0.85e-4 (Sec. 3.2). Note: the v3 abstract of 2504.13154 states A_CB < 1e-4 (95%) and best fit 0.42 +0.40 -0.34 e-4; see prior_measurements.md.
- Bianchini et al. 2020 (arXiv:2006.08061) SPTpol four-point estimator: A_CB < 0.1e-4 (Sec. 3.2).

## Frequency dependence
- Not tested; CMB-lite assumes achromatic CMB signal.

## Systematics caveats
- SPTpol LCDM fit quality is poor; absolute chi2 should be cross-checked with the original Fortran likelihood.
- SPT-3G and BICEP/Keck share the sky mask, so they are correlated (sample variance); joint SPT-3G + SPTpol not done.
- Fixed radio spectral index (-2.65) used in SPTpol compression is lower than other observations suggest.
- Templates computed at Planck 2018 best fit and rescaled (no full recomputation).

## Model / Lagrangian conventions
- No Lagrangian written. Generic statement that any pseudoscalar with a Chern-Simons coupling can source anisotropic rotation. A_CB is in rad^2; the sign of beta is irrelevant for C_L^{alpha alpha}.
