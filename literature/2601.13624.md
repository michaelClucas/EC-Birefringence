# arXiv:2601.13624 - Yin, Du, Li, Zhang, "Joint constraints on cosmic birefringence and early dark energy from ACT, Planck, DESI, and PantheonPlus"

Version read: v2 (05 Aug 2026), full text via arxiv.org/html/2601.13624.

## Data (Sec. 3)
- Planck TT, TE, EE (+ low-l TT, low-l EE, Planck lensing per Table 2) (refs 1907.12875, 1807.06210).
- DESI DR2 BAO; PantheonPlus SNe (1550 SNe). DESY5 deliberately excluded (footnote 1).
- Planck-EB: spectrum analysed by Eskilt & Komatsu (arXiv:2205.13962), 72 data points, 50 <= l <= 1500.
- ACT-EB: Louis et al. DR6 (arXiv:2503.14452), 38 data points, 600 <= l <= 3400 (Sec. 3). Later text says ACT extends to l = 3500 (Fig. 4 discussion); minor inconsistency in the paper.
- Two combinations: "Planck-EB (with Planck+DESI+PantheonPlus)" and "ACT-EB (with Planck+DESI+PantheonPlus)". Cobaya MCMC.
- Frequencies: not stated (they use the published EB band powers; per-frequency handling not discussed).

## Model and conventions (Sec. 2)
- Lagrangian Eq. (1), verbatim in their notation:
  L = -(1/2)(d_mu phi)(d^mu phi) - V(phi) - (1/4) F_{mu nu} F^{mu nu} - (1/4) g phi F_{mu nu} Ftilde^{mu nu}
  with g of mass dimension -1. Metric signature and epsilon sign convention are NOT stated.
- EDE potential Eq. (2): V = Lambda^4 [1 - cos(phi/f)]^n with n = 3; theta = phi/f; theta_i fixed at 2.7; sum m_nu = 0.06 eV.
- Dispersion Eq. (3): omega_pm ~= k -+ (g/2)(dphi/dt + khat . grad phi) = k -+ (g/2) dphi/dt; omega_+ (omega_-) is right (left) handed; right-handed frame with z along propagation.
- beta <-> Delta phi, Eq. (4), verbatim: beta(t) = -(1/2) int_t^{t0} dt' (omega_+ - omega_-) = (g/2)[phi(t0) - phi(t)].
- Sign: positive beta is clockwise on the sky with z along the observer's line of sight; polarization-angle convention follows Komatsu 2022 review (arXiv:2202.13919) (text after Eq. 4).
- Boltzmann Eq. (5) has +-2i beta' term; constant-beta limit Eq. (10): Delta_E +- i Delta_B = e^{+-2i beta}(tilde Delta_E +- i tilde Delta_B). EB Eq. (11): C^EB = (1/2) sin(4 beta)(C^EE - C^BB); Eq. (12) C^EB ~= tan(2 beta) C^EE.
- Computed with CLASS_EDE (Hill et al. arXiv:2003.07355). The rotation is time-dependent across recombination (not a constant beta).

- Goodness of fit vs the 38 ACT-EB points: Planck-EB best fit chi2 = 117.6 (PTE 2.49e-10); ACT-EB best fit chi2 = 53.4 (PTE 0.039) (Sec. 3, Fig. 4 discussion). The Planck-EB-driven model overshoots ACT EB at l ~ 1000-1500.

## Miscalibration alpha / foreground EB
- Not discussed. The paper fits the EB band powers directly with a cosmological model; how alpha was handled in the input EB spectra (Eskilt & Komatsu Planck spectra; ACT DR6 spectra) is not stated. This is an open issue for interpreting their g M_Pl: the ACT DR6 EB is a total (alpha + beta) signal unless alpha is corrected externally.

## Frequency dependence
- Not tested (achromatic Chern-Simons rotation assumed).

## Caveats stated by the authors
- tau_reio ~ 0.135 is much larger than Planck 2018 0.054 +- 0.007 and kSZ/quasar expectations; flagged as a limitation.
- S8 ~ 0.96 is in 5.1-5.2 sigma tension with KiDS-Legacy and 6.7-6.9 sigma with DES Y6; no weak-lensing data included.
- EDE n = 3 and theta_i = 2.7 fixed.
