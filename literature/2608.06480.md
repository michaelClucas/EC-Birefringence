# arXiv:2608.06480 (6 Aug 2026): J. R. Eskilt, "Cosmic birefringence from a joint analysis of ACT and Planck"

Opened by task `work/20261121-042-data-final-fixes` (arXiv abstract and HTML full text). Single author. Rows in `data/birefringence_measurements.csv` with ids `ESK26-*`.

## Result
- Joint ACT DR6 + Planck PR4 (NPIPE) likelihood using auto- and cross-spectra, ACT and Planck instrumental (miscalibration) priors taken at face value: **β = 0.277° ± 0.057°**, 4.8σ (abstract, Sec. III, Sec. IV). Uncertainties are 68% CL throughout (Sec. I).
- Dust-mitigated robustness test (drops 30, 44, 353 GHz): β = 0.236° ± 0.067°, 3.5σ (Sec. III, IV).
- Variants (Sec. III): no Planck α priors 0.280° ± 0.059°; no Planck or ACT α priors 0.442° ± 0.098°; Planck only (dust EB model) 0.387° ± 0.094°; ACT × Planck cross-spectra only 0.178° ± 0.078° (2.3σ); ACT only in this pipeline 0.205° ± 0.072° (0.207° ± 0.073° from published spectra).
- The author says β = 0 would need both the dust EB modelling and the instrumental priors to fail together, and that unresolved systematics must be understood before strong cosmological conclusions.

## Data
- ACT DR6: PA5 and PA6 at f090 and f150, PA4 at f220 (PA4 f150 dropped for failing null tests) (Sec. II).
- Planck PR4 NPIPE: HFI 100, 143, 217, 353 GHz and LFI 30, 44, 70 GHz (Sec. II).
- No WMAP.

## Overlap with combinations already in data/
`DPK25-comb-EK22` (ACT DR6 + WMAP + Planck PR4, 0.264° ± 0.058°) and `DPK25-comb-CG` (ACT DR6 + Cosmoglobe WMAP + Planck) contain the same ACT DR6 and Planck PR4 data. ESK26 is an alternative combination, not an independent measurement: it is never multiplied with them, with DPK25-beta-baseline, or with EK22. `src.likelihood.ISO_DATASETS` gives it components {ACT_DR6, PLANCK}, so `joint()` refuses such products. ESK26 is a joint likelihood (with cross-spectra); the DPK25 numbers are inverse-variance combinations assuming independence. The paper does not compare to 0.264° ± 0.058°.

## Sign convention
Not restated in the paper (EB ∝ tan 4β, β > 0 preferred). Same author and method lineage as EK22 (2205.13962, Minami-Komatsu convention), and the value is positive like ACT and EK22, so it is used in the CONVENTIONS.md sign without change.
