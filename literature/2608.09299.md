## Where the radion-FF̃ coupling comes from (our analysis, checked in work/20261120-009-kk-bounds)
1. **The EM piece is the 5D abelian Chern-Simons form of the KK 1-form n.** With e⁵ = n and T⁵ = dn, the A = B = 5 part of Eq. 39 is ∝ n∧dn∧dn, and n∧dn∧dn = 2φ³ dx⁵∧dA∧dA exactly; the dφ terms cancel [check P2]. The A = B = a part gives the φ-weighted Nieh-Yan term and contains no photon [P3]. So Eq. 41's EM term is "𝒜∧𝓕∧𝓕 with 𝒜 = n": the same structure as our KK-02, with the graviphoton 1-form in place of a bulk U(1) and the radion φ³ in place of the Wilson line.
2. **n∧dn∧dn uses only the metric and the circle direction, not torsion.** n = ζ♭/|ζ| is metric-built from the Killing vector ζ = ∂₅. The same term can be written in Riemannian or Einstein-Cartan KK. Teleparallelism is how they wrote it, not why it is nonzero.
3. **It needs a parity-odd background.** P' = diag(1,-1,-1,-1,-1) is a proper 5D rotation (rotation by π in the (x¹,x²) and (x³,y) planes) [P1]. Under P', radion × F_KK F̃_KK is odd [P4], while φ³F² is even [P5]. So any 5D theory invariant under diffeomorphisms and local Lorentz, on M₄×S¹ with a P'-even background, has no radion-F_KKF̃_KK term at any loop order (the skeptic's argument, confirmed). The 2608 term is diffeomorphism invariant, but evaluated with u = +n it is odd under P' [P7], because P'*n = -n[φ', A'] [P8]. The P'-odd datum is the orientation of u relative to the fiber, i.e. the sign of det(∂_μψ^a) in the Stückelberg vacuum (equivalently, the internal ε_{abcd} plus a vacuum that picks one orientation). Without that background (u absent, or both orientations), the coupling is zero. This is neither a boundary term nor a non-Lagrangian term: it is an explicit ε-term whose odd part is activated by an oriented vector background.
4. **Einstein-Cartan analogue.** In EC (curvature plus spin-sourced torsion), transplanting Eq. 39 as c u∧T^A∧T_A with T = K∧e gives no photon term (K is algebraic and contains no F) [E6]. u∧(Nieh-Yan) gives only a parity-even F² renormalization [E5]. The "u-Holst" term c u∧e^A∧e^B∧R_{AB}(ω) (their vanishing P̂₁, nonzero once there is torsion) does work. Integrating out the contortion gives L ⊃ -(1/8κ₅)(1+12c²)[W² + c_odd W W̃] with W = dn and c_odd = 16c³/(1+12c²) [E7, E8]. So an EC analogue exists where torsion matters, since the Riemannian version is identically zero. It needs the same oriented u, and it is again a radion-driven coupling with the same α link (below).

## Fine-structure constant and bounds
- In their model α ∝ 1/(L₅φ³) (Maxwell coefficient φ³). With L = -¼Z(F² + c_odd FF̃), |β| = (|c_odd|/2)|Δln Z| [R1], so **|Δα/α|_{LSS→0} = 2|β|/|c_odd| = |β|/|c|** for 2608 (c_odd = 2c) [R2]. Their PR4 fit gives Δα/α ≈ +5.2×10⁻³/c at recombination.
- **They apply no α-variation, fifth-force, solar-system or radion-stabilization bound.** Stabilization is explicitly called beyond scope (Sec. II.3). Only the PR4 β and qualitative GW bounds appear.
- Our bounds (work/20261120-009 results.csv): the CMB α bound needs c_odd ≳ 0.68 (c ≳ 0.34). If φ is still rolling, clocks need c_odd ≳ 4 and quasars c_odd ≳ 0.1. The decisive constraint: a radion that rolls after recombination has m ≲ H_* ≈ 3×10⁻²⁹ eV. Their 4D action (L₅φR/2 with no φ kinetic term, Eq. 14/38) is Brans-Dicke with ω = 0, so γ - 1 = -1/2, against Cassini 2.1 ± 2.3×10⁻⁵ (1403.7377 Table 4). The Einstein-frame KK radion has the same gravitational-strength coupling. Evading it needs m > 5×10⁻³ eV (ISL), which is about 10²⁶ H_*, so the field cannot roll after recombination. **The radion × FF̃ route is excluded unless matter couplings of the radion are screened or removed, which the paper does not address.** Torsion does not change this.
