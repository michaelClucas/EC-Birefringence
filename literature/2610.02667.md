# arXiv:2610.02667: Yin & Komatsu, "Distinguishing the origin of cosmic birefringence: dark energy, dark matter, and neutrino asymmetry" (v1, 2 Oct 2026)

Added by task 20261120-032 (iteration 9), answering skeptic 029 item 11. Opened: arXiv abstract page and the arXiv HTML full text (Secs. I-IV), 2026-11-20. Previously listed only as an [ABS] row in `prior_work_torsion_birefringence.md`.

## What it is
- Computes the CMB EB spectrum for a birefringence source coupled to a neutrino-asymmetry current and compares it with axion dark energy and axion dark matter, in one Chern-Simons framework.
- Operator, Eq. (5): L_J = -(1/2)(b_J/M²) J_μ A_ν F̃^{μν}, with J_μ = (Δn_νe, 0). Sec. I notes that a generic conserved current does not make this gauge invariant and adds a term (1/2) g_cs J_μ ∂_ν S^{μν} (Eq. 3) with an antisymmetric S^{μν}. S^{μν} is never written explicitly, so the gauge restoration is asserted, not shown. Reig 2611.07578 (already [KEY] in `prior_work_torsion_birefringence.md`) shows that the gauge-invariant completion is a KR 2-form dual to an axion, and that this route is excluded.
- Source time dependence: Δn ∝ a⁻³ (Eq. 7, relativistic Fermi-Dirac asymmetry, η ≈ 0.249 ξ_νe, Eq. 6). β from Eq. (8): β ∝ g_νe T_γ0³ ∫(1+z)²/H dz, with g_νe = b_J ξ_νe/M².
- Fit (Sec. III): 72 Planck PR4 EB bandpowers, miscalibration prior σ_α = 0.11°, χ² = 65.8 for 70 dof. Result: M/√(b_J ξ_νe) = 44.32 (+12.32, -6.74) TeV (68%). ACT DR6 is cited only in the introduction and not fitted.
- EB shape: the neutrino case is suppressed at ℓ ≲ 20, like axion DM and unlike axion DE. At high ℓ it differs from a constant rotation by Δχ² ≈ 170 (Planck, ℓ = 51-1490) and by ≈ 15 (≈ 10 with miscalibration) for a Simons Observatory forecast.
- No mention of torsion, Einstein-Cartan, teleparallel gravity, Kaluza-Klein or a CFJ vector by name. The closest item is a "purely time-like background" vector (Sec. I).
