# EC-06c repair costs, f(T) frame and GW speed, EC-06d (SNY) at recombination

Conventions: `CONVENTIONS.md`. Every mechanism here is a candidate mechanism. The ACT DR6 β = 0.215° ± 0.074° (2509.13654) is 2.9σ with systematics caveats and may not be cosmological. ## 1. f(T) perturbations: the Lorentz sector fixes the frame, and T_μ is not closed [check_ft_lorentz.py]

Teleparallel f(T), Weitzenböck connection, torsion scalar normalised so that T_sc = +6H² on flat FRW (L2a). Tetrad for scalar modes: a local boost with rapidity η(τ,x) (η = ∂_xζ, ζ the extra Lorentz scalar) applied to the diagonal tetrad e⁰ = a(1+Φ)dτ, e^i = a(1-Ψ)dx^i.

- **Trace vector (L2b):** δT_τ = -3Ψ′ - ∂_xη, δT_x = ∂_x(Φ - 2Ψ) - η′. Hence
  (dT)_{τx} = ∂_x∂_τ(Φ + Ψ) + ∂_x²η - η″ = ∂_x[(Φ+Ψ)′ + ∇²ζ - ζ″].
  At η = 0 this is 022's T5 (L2h). The skeptic's (026) form with ζ is reproduced.
- **ζ equation (L2c-f):** expanding e f(T) to second order and varying η gives an equation proportional to f_TT. For TEGR (f_TT = 0) it vanishes identically: ζ is pure gauge (022's no-go). For f_TT ≠ 0 it fixes ζ uniquely, with no free function:
  **∇²ζ = -3Ψ′ + 3(a″/a′)Ψ - 3(a′/a)(Φ + 2Ψ)** (conformal time; one plane-wave direction).
  f_TT divides out, so the result does not depend on how far f is from TEGR, only on f_TT ≠ 0. ζ is not zero and is non-dynamical at linear order (it is fixed by a constraint, not by a wave equation). This matches the literature statement that in f(T) the Lorentz scalar is constrained, not propagating, on FRW at linear order (Golovnev-Koivisto 1808.05565, abstract only opened). Li-Sotiriou-Barrow 1010.1041 showed that f(T) breaks local Lorentz invariance and that the antisymmetric equations exist (abstract opened). The explicit solution above is derived here; it was not read from those papers. The pure diagonal ansatz (η = 0) would turn the same equation into a constraint on Φ and Ψ, which is why perturbations with that ansatz are over-constrained.
- **dT on solutions (L2g):** in matter domination with static Φ = Ψ = φ₀cos kx, (dT)_{τx} = 15kφ₀ sin(kx)/τ · (1 + 2/(kτ)²) ≠ 0. **dT does not vanish, even where 022's ζ = 0 formula gave zero.** Sub-horizon, for static potentials, |dT|_phys = |C(z)| H |∇Φ| with C = -3 + 3Ḣ/H² (-7.5 in matter domination, -4.4 today). For a source moving at velocity v relative to the cosmic frame, Ψ′ = -v·∇Ψ, and the result is (dT)_{τi} ≈ ∂_i(v·∇Φ), so |dT| ~ vΦ/r². That term dominates locally (N2b): the Sun moves at 370 km/s relative to the CMB frame, and v/(rH) ≫ 1.
  **So U(1) breaks on linear perturbations everywhere: around every moving mass, and in every cosmological era.**
- **Rotations (L1):** rotating the diagonal FRW tetrad by ω(x^μ) about z keeps the metric, T_τ and T_sc, but adds a curl T_i = (-∂_yω, ∂_xω, 0), with (dT)_{xy} = ∇²ω. The f(T) antisymmetric equation contains f_TT T′ ∂_iω (L1b), so f(T) forces ∇ω = 0 at linear order, while TEGR leaves ω free. The CFJ term adds a source (ξ/2)(∂_yK^x - ∂_xK^y) (K^μ = A_νF̃^{μν}) to this same equation (L1d): photons push on the frame. Gravity wins only if f_TT T′ M_P² ≫ ξ|∇×K| (not quantified; open).
- **What selects the frame:** the f(T) antisymmetric equations f_TT S_[μν]{}^ρ ∂_ρT = 0, for any f_TT ≠ 0. On flat FRW they pick the diagonal tetrad (022 T4), so T_t = 3H and ε_T = 3 for any f(T) (the value depends only on H(z)). On perturbations they fix ζ (above) and ω. So **EC-06c is "TEGR-like dynamics (f ≈ T - 2Λ, any tiny f_TT ≠ 0) plus a photon coupling in the frame that f(T)'s Lorentz equations select"**. β needs no deviation of H(z) from ΛCDM, so f(T) background bounds (Nesseris et al. 1308.6142: viable f(T) are practically ΛCDM) do not constrain it. What it does need is f_TT ≠ 0 with f_TT T′ large enough that the frame is set by gravity and not by the photon term. In the f_TT → 0 limit the frame becomes strongly coupled. This is the known f(T) strong-coupling issue, and the photon coupling makes it observable.
- **Proper-frame TEGR (skeptic 026):** the Krššák-Pereira proper-frame prescription selects the diagonal tetrad on flat FRW, so on the background it gives the same T_t = 3H as EC-06c. It does not specify ζ for perturbations. Its natural choice ζ = 0 reproduces 022's T5, which differs from the f(T) solution above. TEGR plus the proper frame and EC-06c therefore agree on FRW and differ off FRW. Neither is closed.
- **Weak static field (L4):** T_sc = +2|∇Φ|² (same sign as 6H²), so T_sc ≈ 6H² + 2|∇Φ|² near masses. This is used in Sec. 2(c).

## 2. Repairs of the broken U(1)

### (a) Stückelberg / Proca photon [check_stueckelberg.py, ec06c_numbers.py]
- S1: L_CFJ(A - ∂σ) = L_CFJ(A) - ¼σ(dp)_{νμ}F̃^{μν} + total derivative, for any p(x). The completion is gauge invariant, and the Stückelberg scalar couples to (dp)·F̃. With canonical π = mσ (mass term -½m²(A - ∂σ)²), the coupling is -(1/4m)π(dp)·F̃: a π-photon mixing that grows as 1/m.
- S3: the mixing element is (dp)k/(2m). Transverse and longitudinal modes are degenerate in vacuum (ω² = k² + m²), so the converted amplitude grows as **A ≈ |dp|L/(4m)** (checked numerically). Over a source of size r: A ≈ ξ[|C|HΦ + vΦ/r]/(4m).
- S2: Proca + constant timelike CFJ gives (ω² - k² - m²)² = p₀²k², so ω²_min = m² - p₀²/4. A mass m > |p|/2 removes the tachyonic band of timelike CFJ (Carroll-Field-Jackiw 1990; Adam-Klinkhamer hep-ph/0101087 report unitarity/causality violation for timelike p). For p ∝ H that band is super-horizon, and its growth is at most exp(1.5ξN) = e^{0.03} over 60 e-folds, so it is harmless even at m = 0. Whether a mass also restores microcausality was not checked.
- Numbers (A < 0.1, a chosen threshold; vacuum mixing, plasma ignored, which is conservative because ω_p ≫ m detunes the mixing): the strongest lower limit comes from photons leaving the solar surface, through the moving-Sun term: **m ≳ 7×10⁻²⁸ eV**. Earth's surface gives 2×10⁻²⁹ eV, cosmological perturbations 5×10⁻³⁴ eV, and CFJ stability at z* gives 1.6×10⁻³² eV. Upper limits: **PDG m_γ < 10⁻¹⁸ eV** (Ryutov 07), and for a Proca/Stückelberg mass (as here) the galactic-field argument **< 10⁻²⁶ eV** (Adelberger-Dvali-Gruzinov hep-ph/0306245; listed but not adopted by PDG). **Window: 7×10⁻²⁸ ≲ m_γ ≲ 10⁻²⁶ eV (or 10⁻¹⁸ eV with PDG only).** It is open, but only by about one decade if the galactic-field limit is accepted.
- Naive EFT cutoff of the induced π-(matter density)-photon contact operator, coefficient ξ/(mM_P²): Λ ~ (mM_P²/ξ)^{1/3} ≈ 20-60 GeV across the Proca window (25 TeV at 10⁻¹⁸ eV). This is power counting only.

### (b) Gravitational Chern-Simons counterterm (Li-Cai-Wang-Zhang 0907.5159)
- What 0907.5159 fixes: for a **fixed external, gradient** p_μ (their p = ∇θ₁, Eq. 1), the photon stress tensor is not conserved, ∇_μT^{μν} = -¼p^νF F̃ (Eq. 5). They add (θ₂/4)RR̃ with θ₂ = q·x, and divergence-matching requires the field constraint q^ν RR̃ = 16πG p^ν F F̃ (Eq. 11). This is a diffeomorphism (Bianchi) problem for a non-dynamical background vector.
- In EC-06c, p = ξT is built from the dynamical tetrad, so diffeomorphism invariance is intact. The inconsistency is the photon one, ∂_νE^ν = -¼(dp)·F̃ (022 G2). That identity involves only photon-sector terms, and no purely gravitational term enters it. **A gravitational CS term cannot repair U(1). (b) is not a repair.** Its constraint qRR̃ ∝ pFF̃ would in addition force RR̃ ≠ 0 wherever photons have E·B ≠ 0.
- Size anyway (N4): the natural coefficient q ~ 16πG p = 10⁻⁹⁷ m, against LAGEOS 1/m_cs ≲ 1000 km (0708.0001, m_cs = 2/q in 0907.5159 normalisation) and GW amplitude birefringence κ ≲ 0.74 Gpc⁻¹ (2101.11153): margins of 10¹⁰³ or more. The bounds allow it, but it does nothing for U(1). 2104.11189 places no dCS bound (abstract).

### (c) Closed replacements of T_μ
- (c1) **ln T_sc · FF̃** (the gauge-invariant completion 022 identified, equal to 2508.10532's ln Q in flat FRW). β = (c/2)[ln T_sc(obs) - ln T_sc(src)] depends only on the endpoints. With T_sc ≈ 6H² + 2|∇Φ|², the observer value is set by the local gravitational field: ln(T_obs/T_*) = -19.8 (cosmological observer), +25.9 (ground, ACT), +11.1 (L2, Planck) (N5). **The sign flips relative to the cosmological value, and ground and L2 differ by a factor 2.3.** The same applies to any ln(curvature/torsion scalar) coupling, including ln Q and ln R (Li-Xia-Li: R = 8πGρ_local inside air). This assumes the f(T)/STEGR scalar near Earth is the weak-field value. Whether 2508.10532 discusses it was not checked. As a torsion completion it is "torsion" only through T_sc, and it carries an environmental dependence that the CMB fits (023) did not include.
- (c2) **Torsion-dragged scalar:** add θ with -(M²/2)(∂θ - T)² and couple -(ξ/4)θFF̃ (closed, so gauge invariant). Equation of motion ∇·[M²(∂θ - T)] = -(ξ/4)FF̃ ≈ 0. On FRW θ̇ = T_t = 3H (decaying mode dropped), and the stress tensor, quadratic in (∂θ - T), **vanishes** on the tracking solution, so there is no EC-03′-type energy cost. Off FRW, θ follows the exact part of T, so local rotations are ~ξΦ. Costs: a new exactly massless scalar (a mass would stop the tracking); M ≥ ξ/g_CAST = 6×10⁶ GeV (CAST g < 5.8×10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹, bounds.csv); inflationary δθ = H_inf/(2πM) gives A_CB = (ξH_inf/4πM)², so M ≥ 2.6×10⁻³ H_inf (SPT-3G A_CB < 1.2×10⁻⁴); an M²T² New-GR-type term of relative size ≤ (M/M_P)² (6×10⁻²⁴ at the CAST minimum). Is it torsion? The photon couples to a scalar, and torsion sets the scalar's rolling rate. It belongs to the ALP class (as KK-02/03), with the background rate fixed by T instead of a potential.

## 3. GW speed in f(T) [check_ft_lorentz.py L3]
δ₁T_sc = 0 and δ₁T_μ = 0 for tensor modes (L3a). The quadratic action gives h″ + (2a′/a + f_TT T̄′/f_T)h′ - ∇²h = 0. **c_T = 1 exactly, for any f(T), on flat FRW** (L3b). Only the friction changes (L3c), so d_GW/d_EM = [f_T(0)/f_T(z)]^{1/2}. This agrees with Cai-Li-Saridakis-Xue 1801.05827 (abstract: GW speed exactly c in f(T)). GW170817 does not bind. The photon term has no linear tensor piece (T_μ unperturbed by h). The f(T) GW-friction bound is not computed; for f ≈ ΛCDM it is small.

## 4. EC-06d: SNY 0⁺ torsion [sny_background.py, check_sny_limit.py]
- **Closure off FRW (Q1):** SNY Eq. 20 is covariant: ∇_μR = -(2/3)(R + 6μ/b)T_μ, with P = Q = 0 (Eqs. 21-23) whenever Y ≡ R + 6μ/b ≠ 0 and spin sources vanish. So **T_μ = -(3/2)∂_μ ln|Y| exactly**: p is closed off FRW, and the CFJ term equals -(3ξ/4)ln|Y|·FF̃ up to a boundary term. Sign: SNY's T_i = T_{ij}{}^j with derivative-first torsion matches our T_μ = T^λ_{μλ}, so T_t = Φ_SNY. Their signature is (-+++), the same as ours.
- **Background at z* (Y1-Y7):** SNY Eqs. 43-47 (matter only, 8πG = 1, a₀ = -1, a₁ = μ + a₀), Table I cases III-V, integrated backward. Checks: case II period 3.63, case III ρ₀ = 0.83, constraint preserved to 10⁻⁵, and ∫Φdt = -(3/2)Δln Y. All three cases reach z* with **Φ/H → 3.00** (the same ε_T = 3 as flat f(T)), β/ξ = 8.4-9.0 rad, and **ξ = (4.2-4.5)×10⁻⁴**. But these histories are torsion-dominated at early times, with H ∝ (1+z)² and **H(z*) = 33-66 × H_ΛCDM(z*)**. They are excluded by the CMB.
- **GR-compatible regime (Q2-Q3):** a heavy 0⁺ mode, s = R/(6μ/b) ≪ 1 with R = ρ_dust. Then ε = (9/2)s/(1+s), β/ξ ≈ ε*/6, and the Friedmann constraint gives H² = (ρ/3)(1 + δ) with δ = 3bρ/8 = (9μ/4)s. So **ξ ≈ 3μβ/δ ≥ 1.1 for |δ(z*)| ≤ 1%** (μ = a₁ - a₀ > 1 because a₁ > 0). In that regime the coupling is the Li-Xia-Li ∂ln R type, with ε* ~ 10⁻²: not O(H) torsion. BBN (radiation is absent from the SNY equations) was not evaluated.
- GW speed: in Eq. 33 only R̄_ij carries second metric derivatives (coefficient a₀ - bR/6). T = ∂ln Y adds friction-type terms, so c_T = 1 is expected. This is argued, not derived.
